# Training Essential — image-64 — Client's Design Idea

Mode: B — Warm + Cool Comedy Triad
Score: 8.5/10
Original file in this repo: `image-64.png`

## What it is

English cartoon.
Top: "After Hearing the" in black, "Client's Design Idea" in white on a red brush banner.
Illustration: young man in a yellow hoodie, bent over, hands on knees, vomiting bright green. Messy dark hair, light stubble, black cargo pants, black smartwatch, white sneakers. Pale lavender blob behind him, lavender-grey shadow under his feet.

## Palette (approximate)

White #FFFFFF about 45–50%, yellow hoodie #FFB800 about 20%, black / dark grey #2F2F33 about 15%, lime green vomit #7ED321 about 8–10%, red banner #D9001B about 5%, pale lavender blob #E6DFF7, skin #F6C193, black outline #111111.

## What it teaches

- Yellow is the hero. Eye goes to the hoodie first after the title.
- Green is the punchline. Green = sick, disgust. Green stream creates a vertical eye path: mouth → drops → puddle.
- Red only for the title banner. White on red is high contrast, but red does not take over.
- Lavender is the cool balance. Pale and low saturation, it separates the man from white without fighting yellow, green, or red.
- Thick black outlines unify everything. White shoes balance dark pants.
- Colour tells the story: yellow = happy designer before, green = sick reaction after, red = alarm.

## Flaws to not repeat

- Six hues is riskier than Mode A. Red and green are complementary and very loud together. They work here only because yellow and white separate them.
- Yellow exclamation marks near his back blend into the hoodie.
- Red banner brush texture makes white text a little less clean on the edge.
- Green is very saturated. Keep it at 8–10%, never more, or it overpowers the yellow.
