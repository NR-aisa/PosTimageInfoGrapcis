# Training Essential — image-63 — Red Quote Card

Mode: A — Single-Dominant Emotional Coloring
Score: 9/10
Original file in this repo: `image-63.png`

## What it is

Bengali quote card.
Top left: "টাটকা রস" in grey.
Top right: red logo.
Main text in red: "অনেক ভুলের হয় না ক্ষমা, বললে স্যরি" — "Many mistakes are not forgiven by saying sorry."
Below it in grey: author "সাবরিন জাওয়াদ ঋতু" (Sabrin Jawad Ritu).
Illustration: angry woman, pointing with one finger, other hand on hip, black hair in a bun, red dress, cream scarf with red flowers, gold bangles and earring.

## Palette (approximate)

White #FFFFFF about 60%, dominant red #E30613 about 25–30%, black #1A1A1E about 8%, skin peach #F7C39A about 5%, cream scarf #FFF3E0, gold accent #F2B705 less than 1%, grey secondary text #8A8D93 about 1–2%.

## What it teaches

- One dominant colour only. Red is the hero.
- Colour matches emotion. Red = anger, warning, no forgiveness.
- Hierarchy: red headline first, red illustration second, grey author name last.
- Repetition: red in headline, logo, dress, flowers, and anger mark.
- Gold only in 3 tiny places (earring + 2 bangles). It does not become a second hero.
- Cream scarf breaks the red dress. White negative space makes red pop.
- No colour fight: no blue, green, or purple competes with red.

## Flaws to not repeat

- Same saturated red for headline, logo, and dress competes a little.
- Grey secondary text is low contrast, especially on mobile.
- Red anger mark on black hair almost disappears.
- Whole image is warm with no cool balance.
